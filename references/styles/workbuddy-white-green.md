# WorkBuddy White-Green Visual PPT Preset

Use this preset when the user asks for WorkBuddy brand style, white-green, teal-green, fresh
product style, or a clean light deck for Tencent WorkBuddy training / product material.

## Visual Identity

- Base: pure white `#FFFFFF` and off-white paper `#F7FBF9`.
- Brand accent: teal-green `#0AC89E` (primary), deep teal `#078E71` (hover / emphasis),
  mint `#C7F2E6` (soft fills), pale green `#E2F8F3` (light panels and section washes).
- Ink: near-black `#12140F` for titles and body text; secondary text `#5A6B62`; hairlines `#D9E5E0`.
- Accent for emphasis only: a small amount of lime `#D8F238` or warm gray is allowed, one spot per slide.
- Overall mood: clean, precise, product-grade, calm, information-rich, slightly technical.
- Lighting: flat to softly lit; subtle depth, thin borders, crisp shadows. No heavy glass blur.
- Avoid: purple-blue gradients, orange, beige, dark themes, neon green body text on white,
  large green gradient washes.

## Layout Patterns

- Cover: light composition with a strong title, a refined product/agent scene, fewer dense modules.
- Inner slide: high-density information architecture, like a polished product-education infographic.
- Recommended composition:
  - top title bar with compact subtitle or page marker
  - left or center grid of white cards with thin `#D9E5E0` borders and pale-green section washes
  - right-side 3D illustration, agent/workbench scene, UI panel, data model, or abstract system diagram
  - bottom strip for source, takeaway, or flow summary
- Keep the slide full-bleed. Do not draw a slide thumbnail inside another frame.
- Prefer clear boundaries and hard structure over soft gradient blobs.

## Information Density

- Inner pages should feel like finished business/product infographics, not minimal keynote slides.
- Use numbered cards, metric chips, timelines, swimlanes, quadrant grids, layered architecture
  diagrams, comparison tables, or mini dashboards.
- Let diagrams carry information instead of relying on one large paragraph.
- Keep enough whitespace for polish, but avoid large empty regions.

## Common Visual Ingredients

- White cards with thin gray-green borders, small teal-green header bars, and tiny teal index numbers.
- Grid lines, dotted connection paths, blueprint-like structure, subtle data streams.
- Subtle 3D icons: document stacks, spreadsheet grids, slides, calendar, clock, lock, cloud,
  database, plug / USB connector, gear, chat bubble, agent avatar.
- Semi-realistic desk / workbench / cockpit scenes when relevant, rendered clean and light.
- Thin iconography and small labels for a dense professional feel.

## Typography Guidance

- Clean modern Chinese typography; deep ink `#12140F` main text.
- Strong hierarchy: large title, compact section heads, smaller body labels.
- Put critical copy in high-contrast panels (white card or pale green bar with ink text).
- Never put small text in light green on white — contrast is insufficient.
- If source content is long, add more panels instead of shrinking text into illegibility.

## Avoid

- Plain PPT-like bullet pages.
- Sparse pages with one big icon and a few words.
- Decorative gradient blobs or unrelated abstract backgrounds.
- Fake logos, fake citations, invented customer names, or invented source names.
- Brand-specific colors, names, or marks from an earlier project unless explicitly supplied.
- Any real product screenshot, QR code, or person's face.

## Prompt Reminder

Ask for a “complete finished 16:9 presentation slide image” and describe the full slide composition.
Explicitly state that it is not a poster mockup, not a screenshot of PowerPoint, and not a layout
made from plain text boxes.
