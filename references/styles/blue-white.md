# Blue-White Visual PPT Preset

Use this preset for high-density, polished business slides generated as complete raster images.

## Visual Identity

- Main palette: white, ice blue, pale cyan, light steel blue, deep navy, and blue-gray.
- Accent palette: small touches of electric blue, teal, silver, or warm yellow for emphasis only.
- Overall mood: premium, clear, modern, calm, and information-rich.
- Lighting: soft studio light, translucent panels, subtle depth, and crisp shadows.
- Avoid dark themes, beige-heavy themes, brown/orange palettes, and purple-blue gradient dominance.

## Layout Patterns

- Cover: atmospheric composition with a strong title, refined background scene, and fewer dense modules.
- Inner slide: high-density information architecture similar to a polished consulting infographic.
- Recommended composition:
  - top title bar with compact subtitle or page marker
  - left or center grid of glass cards, tables, metrics, short bullets, or callouts
  - right-side 3D illustration, dashboard scene, data model, map, product workflow, or abstract system visual
  - bottom strip for source, quote, key takeaway, or timeline
- Keep the slide full-bleed. Do not draw a slide thumbnail inside another frame.
- Use depth and background imagery to support the topic while keeping text zones readable.

## Information Density

- Inner pages should feel like finished business infographics, not minimal keynote slides.
- Use structured elements such as numbered cards, metric chips, timelines, swimlanes, quadrant grids, layered architecture diagrams, comparison tables, or mini dashboards.
- Let diagrams carry information instead of relying on a single large paragraph.
- Keep enough whitespace for polish, but avoid large empty regions.

## Common Visual Ingredients

- Glassmorphism cards with thin blue borders.
- Dotted maps, grid lines, blueprints, data streams, and interface panels.
- Subtle 3D icons: document stacks, nodes, agent avatars, buildings, network graphs, charts, compass, lock, cloud, and database.
- Semi-realistic business or technology scenes when relevant: command center, research desk, collaboration wall, data cockpit, service workflow, or city/community system.
- Thin iconography and small labels for a dense professional feel.

## Typography Guidance

- Use clean modern Chinese typography with deep navy main text.
- Use strong hierarchy: large title, compact section heads, and smaller body labels.
- Put critical copy in high-contrast panels.
- Avoid overly tiny body text. If source content is long, create more panels instead of shrinking everything into illegibility.

## Avoid

- Plain PPT-like bullet pages.
- Sparse pages with one big icon and a few words.
- Decorative gradient blobs or unrelated abstract backgrounds.
- Fake logos, fake citations, invented customer names, or invented source names.
- Brand-specific colors, names, or marks from an earlier project unless explicitly supplied for the current deck.
- Cropped, blurry, dark, or stock-like images that do not help explain the page.

## Prompt Reminder

Ask for a “complete finished 16:9 presentation slide image” and describe the full slide composition. Explicitly state that it is not a poster mockup, not a screenshot of PowerPoint, and not a layout made from plain text boxes.
